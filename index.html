Hands-Free Voice Commands

  • "Mark pin" Override GPS pin / green
  • "Hole 7" Jump to a mapped hole
  • "Where am I?" Hear course vs home
  • "Par 3 / 4 / 5" Set hole par rating
  • "Add stroke" / "Count shot" Add 1 stroke to hole
  • "Undo stroke" Remove last stroke
  • "Next hole" / "Finish hole" Save hole score & advance
  • "Skip hole" Advance without saving strokes
  • "Finish round" Save full round history card
  • "What's my score?" Hear score relative to par
  • "Okay Caddie" / "Distance" Get club & target advice
  • "Good shot" / "Hit green" Log hit & add 1 stroke
  • "Came up short" / "Flew long" Tune club yardages
